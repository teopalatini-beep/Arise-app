ARISE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

ARISE ("the App", "we", "our") is a 90-day personal-development challenge app developed by Mateo Palatini. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the App's core functionality:

  • Account data: email address and password (hashed) used to create or log in to your account via Supabase Auth.
  • Progress data: daily mission completions, points, journal entries, streaks, and badges you record inside the App.
  • Device data: anonymous device identifiers used by Expo/React Native for crash reporting and push notification delivery.
  • Push notification token: if you grant notification permission, we store a device token to deliver your scheduled reminders.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any sensitive personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and sync your progress across devices.
  • To send daily motivational push notifications at times you configure.
  • To restore your progress if you reinstall the App or switch devices.
  • To improve App stability using anonymous crash/error logs.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your progress data is stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage) and optionally synced to Supabase (supabase.com), a cloud database provider that complies with industry-standard security practices including TLS encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Row-level security rules ensure that only you can access your own data.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — database and authentication (Privacy Policy).
  • Expo / React Native — app runtime and push notification infrastructure (Privacy Policy).

5. Data Retention

Your account and progress data are retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. We will fulfill deletion requests within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

ARISE is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Push Notifications

Push notifications are entirely opt-in. You may disable them at any time in your device's Settings or inside the App's notification settings screen. Disabling notifications does not affect any other App functionality.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to or restrict processing of your data.
  • Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
